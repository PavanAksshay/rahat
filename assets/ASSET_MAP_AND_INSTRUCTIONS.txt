RAHAT INAUGURATION ASSET PACKAGE
================================

Purpose
-------
This folder contains the real visual assets supplied for the Rahat club inauguration presentation.
Rahat is a student-led club and media house at Manipal Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, focused on mental well-being.
Established 2023.

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS FOR ANTIGRAVITY
--------------------------------------
1. Use the supplied real photographs/videos as the primary visual material.
2. Do NOT replace them with stock photos or AI-generated people/events.
3. Preserve the authenticity of the photographs; do not heavily retouch faces.
4. Use the official logo in 00_LOGO exactly as supplied. Do not redraw or modify it.
5. Video files are in 11_VIDEOS. Several are vertical. Never stretch them horizontally.
6. The folder names indicate the event/context and should be used to select assets.
7. Do not randomly mix event-specific assets unless the storyboard calls for a montage.
8. Do not invent statistics, testimonials, collaborations or event details.

DESIGN DIRECTION
----------------
Core concept: RAHAT — MAKING SPACE

The presentation should feel like:
- premium editorial design
- documentary storytelling
- cinematic event recap
- human and emotionally intelligent

It should NOT feel like:
- generic college-club PowerPoint
- corporate annual report
- Canva template
- clinical mental-health presentation

Suggested base palette:
- warm ivory #F7F2EA
- deep navy/near-black #080D1C
- Rahat red/pink #E7193F

Use restrained accents of dusty pink, lavender, pale blue, beige and occasional yellow from the real photos.

Typography: elegant high-contrast serif for major statements + clean sans-serif for supporting information.

Prefer full-bleed photos, asymmetric editorial layouts, large typography, overlaps and generous negative space. Avoid repetitive 2x2 grids, excessive rounded cards, generic icons, gradients, 3D objects and cliché mental-health imagery.
